The Origin Story

Director of Policy & Legal Operations, Ford Motor Company · Law Professor

It started with a spreadsheet and a husband.

I'm a working mom, a Director of Policy & Legal Operations at Ford Motor Company, and a law school professor who moonlights as the sole keeper of every single thing our family needed to remember, do, schedule, and follow up on. The mental load is real, and I was carrying all of it.

I started in Claude Chat by mapping out everything I actually do: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly. An honest, unsexy inventory of the invisible work. The goal was to split it with my husband Chris.

Chris's response? "This is great. Can you just send me a list every day?"

My response: absolutely not. But also... wait. Could I automate that?

That question is what became Claudette. Claude Chat suggested I move into Claude Cowork, where I connected my Gmail, Google Calendar, and a Notes app on my Mac. No coding. Just describing — in plain English — exactly what I needed.

Claudette was featured in Business Insider in June 2026. Since then, my inbox has been full of people asking: how? This page is my answer.

"I'm not gonna give you a list every day."

So I built something that does it for me. At 4am. Every single morning. — Whitney Stefko Dover

The Goods

The Core Prompt

This is my original system prompt — the foundation Claudette runs on. I've iterated on this a lot since the beginning, and yours will look different for your family. But this is the architecture. Copy it, adapt it, make it yours. The specificity is what makes it work.
WHO YOU ARE

You are Whitney's personal Chief of Staff. You are organized, warm, and proactive. Your job is to make sure Whitney is fully prepared for her day and week — as a mother, household manager, and professional — before she wakes up. You have access to her Gmail, Google Calendar, and her Mac Note titled "Dover Household Tasks."

STEP 1 — REVIEW & UPDATE GOOGLE CALENDAR

Check Whitney's Gmail and add relevant events if not already on the calendar. Categories to watch:

  • School, camp & kids' activities — add with 10-min reminder
  • Birthday parties — add party + RSVP reminder (3 weeks out) + gift reminder (1 week out)
  • Bills & utilities — add due date + "pay by" reminder 5 days prior
  • Appointments (doctors, cleaners, services) — add with 10-min reminder
  • Auto-orders & subscriptions — add expected delivery date

STEP 2 — REVIEW GOOGLE CALENDAR

Review today's full calendar + remaining days this week. Google Calendar is the source of truth.

STEP 3 — REVIEW DOVER HOUSEHOLD TASKS NOTE

Read the Mac Note titled "Dover Household Tasks." Pull: daily/weekly/monthly tasks, Sara's schedule (au pair), Whitney's work commitments affecting family schedule, and any one-offs or time-sensitive notes.

STEP 4 — COMPOSE THE MORNING BRIEFING

Send email to Whitney at 4:00 AM PT daily. Subject: "☀️ Your Dover Briefing — [Day], [Month Date]"

Structure: Affirmation → Daily Notes & To-Dos → Weekly Heads Up → For Chris & Sara (with ready-to-send iMessage drafts for each).

Flags: ⚡ time-sensitive · 👶 kids-related · 💰 financial. Keep it scannable — bullets, not paragraphs. Max 3 minutes to read. Tone: warm, capable, like a trusted chief of staff who has everything handled.

✦ This prompt took months of iteration. Yours should reflect your family's structure, tools, and rhythms. Rename "Dover Household Tasks" to your own note. Swap Chris and Sara for your people. The logic is the template — the specifics are yours to fill in.

The How-To

How to Build Yours

1

Do the mental load audit in Claude Chat

Before you touch any tool, open Claude.ai and ask it to help you map everything you do for your household — daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly. Be exhaustive. This becomes the content of your household tasks note, and it's how you teach your agent what "everything" actually means.

Prompt to start: "Help me create a comprehensive list of everything I manage for my household. Ask me questions to make sure we capture all of it — daily, weekly, monthly, and one-off tasks."
2

Create your Household Tasks note

In Apple Notes (or Google Docs, Notion — whatever you'll actually maintain), create a note with a specific title like Dover Household Tasks. Structure it clearly: Daily Tasks, Weekly Tasks, Monthly Tasks, Au Pair Schedule, Work Commitments This Week, Notes & One-Offs.

This note is Claudette's "source of truth" for everything not on the calendar. Keep it updated — she reads it every day.

3

Open Claude Cowork on your Mac

Download Claude Desktop at claude.ai/download. Open it, then switch to the Cowork tab. This is where your agent lives — it can access your computer, your apps, and your connected accounts.

Work email workaround: If your work email (e.g. Outlook) can't be connected directly, go into Outlook settings and add your personal Gmail as a forwarding address for relevant categories — school, bills, anything you want Claudette to see.
4

Connect Gmail + Google Calendar

In Cowork, connect your personal Gmail and Google Calendar. These are Claudette's two main data sources. The connection takes about 2 minutes — you'll authorize via your Google account.

Make sure the calendar your family events actually live on is the one you connect.

5

Paste in the prompt and customize it

Copy the system prompt above. In Cowork, paste it into your agent's instructions. Then personalize it: replace my family's names with yours, update the note title, adjust the send time, add or remove bill categories that apply to your life.

Pro tip: Don't try to make it perfect on day one. Run it for a week, see what's missing or wrong, and refine. Iteration is the whole game.
6

Run it, refine it, make it yours

Trigger a test run and read the output critically. Is anything missing? Is something flagged that shouldn't be? Is the tone right? Every "that's not quite right" is a prompt refinement. The prompt above represents months of small edits.

The goal isn't perfection. It's progress. Even an 80% briefing is better than no briefing.
